The Problem: Capability without scaffolding

Frontier models have immense analytical capability, but when interacting with neurodivergent users, raw intelligence is not enough. Without structured pacing, interactions easily spiral into cognitive overload, decision paralysis, and hyperfocus exhaustion.

Paperkinn bridges the gap between frontier models and cognitive realities, transforming open-ended tool callers into dependable, emotionally intelligent partners.

Architecture & Experimental Scaffolding

Adaptive Pacing & Cadence

Experimental heuristic tracking interaction velocity. Proactively introduces concise outputs and scheduled pause checkpoints when fatigue or hyperfocus risk increases.

Cognitive Load Reduction

Transforms complex, open-ended operational branches into single-action defaults and structured next steps to reduce executive-function overhead.

Tiered Boundary Checks

Structured operational constraints designed to distinguish productive flow from extended session fatigue, reinforcing user-defined rest intervals.

Contextual Continuity

Session-aware memory indexing via MCP integration that maintains persistent project context and working-memory anchors across agent runs.
